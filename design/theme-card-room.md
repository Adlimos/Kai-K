# Card Room

A custom theme made with theme-factory for Felt Lab: a private card room after midnight. Deep spruce walls, baize felt, mahogany rail, ivory card stock and one brass accent. None of the ten preset themes fit a poker table, so this one was generated for the brief.

## Color Palette

- **Spruce Room**: `#0e211b` - page background
- **Panel**: `#13291f` / `#1a3529` - raised surfaces
- **Baize**: `#1d6a4e` to `#0c3c2c` - the table felt (radial)
- **Mahogany Rail**: `#3a1f15` - table rail
- **Card Stock Ivory**: `#efe6d2` - primary text, card faces `#f8f2e4`
- **Brass**: `#d6a94e` - primary actions, highlights, the single accent
- **Verdigris**: `#63c9a0` - correct answers
- **Oxblood**: `#9e2638` / `#ec7a87` - wrong answers, penalties
- **Range actions**: raise `#d4574a`, call `#e3b552`, fold = panel
- **Four-colour deck**: spades `#1c1c1c`, hearts `#b4233a`, diamonds `#2c5aa8`, clubs `#2a7a4b`

## Typography

- **Headers and numbers**: Gloock (high-contrast serif, reads like a card index)
- **Body and UI**: Instrument Sans, tabular figures on

## Best Used For

Poker study tools, card-game interfaces, casino-adjacent products that want to feel crafted rather than flashy.
